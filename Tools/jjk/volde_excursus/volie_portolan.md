# JJK Portolan — What Each Door Does

Read this before you drive a `jjx_` verb you have not driven this session, and
whenever the question is what a door *returns* rather than what it *takes*. It
is an **excursus** (`jjsuc_excursus`): held out of the standing session load and
read on demand, reached from the trigger line beneath the MCP Command Reference
in `Tools/jjk/volis_jjk.md`.

A portolan is the written sailing directions that accompany a chart. The chart
marks each harbour; the portolan says what you find on entering one. The command
reference is the chart — every command's params stand there and are the
contract, an unknown field being named back to you with the fields the door
expected. What each door then *does* stands here: what prints inline and what
rides the gazette, what a param elects or filters, what the door announces, and
what it deliberately leaves alone.

## What stayed on the chart

Three classes of sentence kept their seat in the command reference rather than
moving here, because no refusal will tell you about them after the fact:

- a **destructive default** — an omitted param that widens the act, as
  `jjx_record` with no `files` does, and the whole-body replaces at `jjx_curry`,
  `jjx_rodear` and `jjx_enface`;
- a **silent loss** — work that goes with every door reporting success: the
  cocket guard, a settled pace's demoted notch, a refit voiding criteria already
  driven, `jjx_rasp`'s normalize;
- a **fence** — an act that is the operator's to direct, which the equerry
  performs willingly and nothing refuses: `jjx_create`, `jjx_archive`,
  `jjx_rodear`.

Refusals themselves moved here, and that is the one place this file's shape
departs from a plain size cut. A refusal fires loudly, names what it expected
and names its own remedy, so the door corrects you at the moment the correction
is cheap. A silent loss has no such moment. When the resident block could not
hold both classes under the composed-size ceiling, the loud half moved and the
quiet half stayed.

## How to read an entry

One heading per command, in the command reference's own order, carrying that
command's prose exactly as it stood in the fenced block — a passage moved whole,
nothing reworded in transit, one sentence per line.

Five commands have no entry: `jjx_list`, `jjx_reorder`, `jjx_relocate` and
`jjx_drop` never carried prose beyond their signatures, and `jjx_create` carries
only its fence, which stayed resident.

## `jjx_enroll`

THE ONE BIRTH DOOR — every pace is born here, and a jjezs_slate notice staged anywhere else is refused back to it.
silks+docket+intent via gazette_in.md only; sire = the sire's HANDLE (jj, rb) and never an infield clone dirname, omitted to inherit the sire the heat's live paces agree on — where that election cannot settle the slate refuses, naming the case, the declared handles and the remedy.
A sire giving a heat its SECOND sire is legitimate and passes SILENTLY here; the sire-span warning fires at jjx_alter's atajo declaration, never at the slate that would merely reveal one.
kind = "hackamore" (slates a lope), "poort" (slates a pace that holds the pass) or "entrepas" (slates a pace born, worked and closed in this one dispatch) — the birth door for the kind, omitted births an ordinary pace, and for "entrepas" it is the ONLY door, jjx_whorl refusing that value in both directions.
predecessor_coronets = THE PACES THE BORN PACE WAITS ON, a NATIVE ARRAY, cross-heat licensed, and THE ADDRESSING IS THE PLAIN ONE, unlike jjx_hopple's inversion: the pace being born IS the pace waiting.
OPTIONAL here where the delta doors require their list, a slate naming no predecessor being the ordinary pace.
Every body must resolve to a LIVE pace and none may be named twice, else the WHOLE birth refuses in the delta doors' own words, before anything is minted.
crupper = a BOOLEAN, births the pace carrying the heat-grain wait: it waits on the rest of the heat it is being born into.
It takes no identity of any kind, because the only heat it could mean is this call's own firemark, and it passes no whole-set judgment — it resolves nothing.
THE SELF-REFERENCE THE SET CANNOT CONSTRUCT, THIS ONE CAN: a predecessor body cannot name the coronet being minted below it, where the HEAT stands before the birth does.
readable_sires = the sires the born pace may READ and never write — its read list, the other half of its atajo, a NATIVE ARRAY spelled as jjx_alter's read half is because it IS that declaration one grain down.
Three states, the store's own: absent is UNDECLARED and stands NO read-only peer, there being no fallback to the heat's own read half (a heat's lists are permission bounding what its paces may declare; this one is the manifest of what THIS pace will read); [] is DECLARED EMPTY and says the same thing on purpose.
Every handle passes the pedigree gate, and the whole list passes the atajo pace gate against the heat it is born into — so a slate refuses for naming a sumpter the heat does not admit, or for naming its own write sire twice

## `jjx_alter`

NO GAZETTE — every field is a param, and a staged body refuses rather than being discarded.

Every declared handle is gated against the pedigree registry at the door.
THE SIRE-SPAN WARNING FIRES HERE and nowhere else — non-gating, emitted when a declaration GROWS the write atajo past one sire (a re-declaration of an atajo that already spanned says nothing new), naming what changes: a firemark-aimed lunge refuses from there, and every later slate into that heat must pass an explicit sire

## `jjx_gesta`

THE WRAP HARVEST: which paces CLOSED, newest first, and what each close's chalk said.
Reads the JOURNAL'S OWN RECORD LINE and that is complete rather than partial — a wrap journals ALWAYS and reaches a sire trunk only where the converge composes a commit — so it owes no cross-tree dedup, which is what parts it from jjx_log's spanning read (that door wants notches too, and a notch IS a work commit).
ONE CLOSE IS ONE WRAP, and the line is named to make it so: the store's clone carries the CORPUS line beside the record line, and a wrap that converged a vulgate lands there a commit built from that wrap's own chalk, so a read across every ref returns a second entry per converging close — same coronet, same instant, no summary and no trailers, its commit the corpus trunk's position.
EVERY COUNT THIS DOOR REPORTS IS IN WRAPS, never rows, and exactly one line reports it: the ceiling line where the inline table clipped, a plain count line otherwise.
Two channels on jjx_log's own shape: the inline result is a BOUNDED INDEX — one row per wrap carrying timestamp, commit, coronet, heat, silks and the close's headline clipped, newest 50 rows — and the gazette carries every harvested wrap whole under one lede-less jjezs_gesta notice, each entry its full summary THEN THE W CHALK'S TRAILERS VERBATIM.
THE TRAILERS ARE THE POINT and they are passed through rather than parsed: `Spook: none` RENDERS AS IS and is never elided, its presence being what says the friction question was put and answered; `Warrant:`, `Warrant-criteria:`, `Converge:` and `Vulgate:` ride the same way.
since = a STAMP, the studbook position every fettle prints, making the harvest a DELTA so a repeated read is cheap; limit bounds the no-stamp first call and counts WRAPS, the election having narrowed before it bounds.
heats = a NATIVE ARRAY of firemarks, any input form, on the fettle's own shape: omitted and [] are ONE store-wide verdict, and an element naming no heat REFUSES THE WHOLE CALL rather than answering half of it, since a half-honored election hands back a short harvest indistinguishable from a quiet heat.
An elected call carries only wraps the live store can still place, so a close whose heat has retired answers no election and rides the store-wide read alone.
trailers = render the trailer block ALONE: the gazette keeps each stanza's header line and its trailer block byte-for-byte, `Spook: none` included, and withholds THE SUMMARY AND NOTHING ELSE — the form a friction pass reads, the trailers having no inline channel at all.
A stamp naming no position in this journal is REPORTED and the bounded default harvested — never an error, never a silent full dump — and that line stands AHEAD of the rows whose meaning it changes.
An empty delta SAYS so rather than printing a bare header.
A wrap whose coronet the live store no longer holds renders with the coronet alone: a retired heat's entries stand in the journal forever

## `jjx_search`

result terse, gazette always populated.
The inline result is a BOUNDED INDEX of the result document: the commonest matching words, the strongest rows, each row clipped; every bound that fires names how many of how many it showed.
The gazette carries the SAME document whole under one lede-less jjezs_harbourage notice — every summary entry, every row, excerpts unclipped, composed by the same renderer, so the index is that document read short rather than a second rendering.
ALWAYS populated, not only above the inline ceiling, and empty-bodied where the pattern matched nothing.
actionable filters both halves identically, AND NO WARRANT ROW CAN PRINT UNDER IT: a warrant rides an indult pace where the filter keeps rough ones, and the filter asks what may be STARTED, which licence for already-designated work does not bear on.
THE PACE FIELDS SEARCHED ARE silks, docket AND WARRANT, in that order, so a warrant hit prints `warrant:` and NEVER folds into `docket:` — a docket is living authority a reslate moves, a warrant frozen licence only a release and re-bridle can touch, and a reader acting on a stale citation must know which they hold.
Warrant is ordered LAST so that within one tier the docket row wins and the frozen text never masks the living one.
Every state the actionable filter does not exclude is searched, a SETTLED pace's warrant included, which persists past the close as the record of what its executing session was told

A retirement with none staged REFUSES; the body lands in the archive commit, which is its only permanent home, the heat leaving the live store in the same act.

## `jjx_enface`

WRITE that document from a LEDE-LESS gazette_in.md jjezs_vademecum notice (the header is `# jjezs_vademecum` ALONE), non-empty body, whole-body replace: seeding, revising and rewriting are all just "the staged body differs from what stands".
GATED ON THE COCKET, the paddock's guard over the registry's addressing: stage the LEDE-LESS jjezs_cocket notice the read emitted beside the body — lede-less here where the paddock's carries a firemark, there being one document for it to mark — and a document that MOVED since your read REFUSES, naming both marks and prescribing a fresh read; the equerry merges nothing and nothing is written.
Lands ONE commit of its own carrying no other mutation; note appends to that message, which is where a ruling's rationale lives and its ONLY home — the document states what is ruled, git alone journals why.
A body equal to what stands lands no commit and succeeds.
An empty body refuses rather than blanking.
OPERATOR-DIRECTED ONLY — the Rodear Protocol's fence exactly: the drover proposes a ruling and never enfaces one unbidden, and the absence of a confirm gate is not permission

## `jjx_redocket`

THE DOCUMENT DOOR, CARRYING ONE KIND OF DOCUMENT: one or more jjezs_reslate notices in gazette_in.md, each lede a coronet and each body a whole docket, all landing in ONE commit — mass reslate is what it is for.
A jjezs_paddock or jjezs_slate notice staged here REFUSES BY NAME, naming jjx_curry and jjx_enroll, and that refusal fires AHEAD of every shape check — a wrong door is not a wrong notice.
One heat only: every coronet's parent must name the same heat, else rejected, the run landing one heat-affiliated commit; order among the notices is irrelevant, each naming its own target.
What one-door-one-document costs is ONE-COMMIT ATOMICITY ACROSS KINDS — a paddock revision, a reslate run and a slate land as three cheap commits — accepted on the record, not an oversight.
before/after/first ARE RETIRED HERE and a call naming one REFUSES, naming jjx_enroll; the field is still parsed only so that refusal can reach you rather than an unknown-field complaint

## `jjx_whorl`

THE SCALAR PACE EDITOR — one params door for the four authored pace fields, no gazette and no parser.
At least one field required; a call naming none is refused with the vocabulary.
kind: three readings — a kind word ("hackamore"/"poort") declares it, an explicit JSON null clears it, absent is no instruction.
"entrepas" IS REFUSED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS, neither refusal offering a remedy on that pace and both naming the exit, which is a new pace.
Asked on the MOVE, so re-declaring the standing kind stays the silent no-op every unchanged field here is.
predecessor_coronets IS RETIRED HERE and a call naming it — an empty array included — REFUSES, naming jjx_hopple and jjx_unhopple; the field is still parsed only so that refusal can reach you instead of an unknown-field complaint.
readable_sires = the pace's read list, REPLACED WHOLE — same key and same three states as jjx_enroll's and jjx_alter's: absent is silence and leaves the stored list standing, [] declares a list that reads nothing, a list replaces the whole.
There are NO add/remove doors and the predecessor set's retirement does not reach it: that answered a stale read over a large many-authored set, and a one-or-two-handle list one operator sets on one pace has nothing for it to bite on.
THE VOIDING TABLE, enforced by field: sire, readable_sires and kind void a designation (as the docket does at jjx_redocket) — the read list for the sire's own reason, that it decides what a saddle provisions; silks and predecessors never do — and a void is ANNOUNCED, naming the tier it discarded.
Refuses per field on a settled pace: sire, readable_sires and kind are provenance, silks stays editable

## `jjx_hopple`

So one call fans a wait across many dependents and never reads back a set it is not changing, which is why this takes deltas and never a whole-set write (JJS0 `jjdpm_predecessors`).
TWO GRAINS ON THE SUBJECT AXIS, ONE ON THE TARGET AXIS: a CORONET writes the target's predecessor set, a FIREMARK writes each target's `crupper` — the mark saying it waits on the rest of its own heat (JJS0 `jjdpm_crupper`) — and only a pace can wait, so a firemark among the TARGETS refuses at either grain, naming the subject axis as where a heat belongs.
At heat grain every target must stand in the subject heat, which is the inside-only law made mechanical: a crupper holds no identity, so an inter-heat wait stays pace-grained.
Lists are NATIVE ARRAYS, cross-heat licensed at pace grain, required.
EVERY CALL REPORTS THE RESULTING STATE of every target it was given, moved or not, at the grain that moved — the standing double check.
Refusals are WHOLE-CALL and write nothing: a target that names the subject, one named twice, one no pace answers, one already resolved (spent plan), one standing outside the subject heat at heat grain, and — hopple only — a subject that has landed or been abandoned, or a retired heat, since nothing can wait on it.
Advisories, never failures: an add already standing, a remove of a wait never held.
A CITATION BINDS THE PACE IT NAMES, AND THE BINDING ENDS WHEN EITHER PACE RESOLVES: hoppling holds the subject in the live store while both stand live, so authoring one is a commitment — but THE GRAPH HOLDS LIVE PACES ONLY, so a wrap lifts the citation from every pace waiting on it and clears its own set, and a drop clears its own set, every removal announced by the ceremony that performed it.
A wait you authored can therefore be gone without anyone having lifted it by hand: that is the model working, not an edit you missed.
THE MARK IS NOT A CITATION and none of that reaches it — it names nothing, so nothing can strand it, and it follows a relocated pace to its new heat with no door maintaining it.
NEITHER DOOR VOIDS A DESIGNATION, at either grain.
No size_limit: both are structural mutators

## `jjx_unhopple`

the mirror of jjx_hopple above, same shape and same refusals at both grains, minus the subject-state gate — a wait naming a resolved pace or a retired heat must stay liftable, because that is exactly the predecessor gate's remedy: one call, identity = the departing pace, remove_coronets = the dependents the interdictum listed

## `jjx_coronets`

coronet IDs in heat order, one per line, inline — no silks, no docket.
Tags follow the coronet, which stays the first token: an abandoned pace tags "[abandoned]", an indult one "[indult <tier>]", an ABEYANT one "[abeyant ₢<trigger>]" (the trigger folds into the state tag as a bridle's tier does), an UNSETTLED lope "[hackamore]".
A kind either CORRECTS the state word or ADDS to it: hackamore REPLACES it while it would mislead, and only while — the kind survives the close, so a settled lope reads its own state again and joins the additive group ("[complete]  [hackamore]"); poort and entrepas ADD their own group AFTER any state tag, both facts standing ("[indult opus]  [poort]"), and a rough pace carrying one shows the kind alone ("[entrepas]") since rough has no state tag of its own.
A kind group never stands alone on a resolved pace, a bare kind reading as live work.
A dam tags "[dam <coronet>]" last.
remaining includes indult, abeyant and every kind; rough excludes indult, abeyant, hackamore and entrepas — the kinds that admit no designation at all — but KEEPS poort, which is designable

## `jjx_runnable`

WHAT CAN START NOW, store-wide, inline (no gazette).
Partitions every LIVE pace into RUNNABLE and BLOCKED on the graph axis ALONE — kind, tier and heat status annotate rows and NEVER decide buckets, so a lope with nothing before it is runnable and says so.
A pace is RUNNABLE when no body in its predecessor set RESOLVES to a live pace: each body is resolved, never the set counted, so an edge onto work that already landed holds nothing.
A blocked row names the NEAREST blockers only — never the transitive closure.
heats is a NATIVE ARRAY of firemarks and a ROW ELECTION, never a filter: the walk is store-wide whatever is elected, so a blocker in an UNELECTED heat is still named on the elected row that waits on it; omitted and [] are one verdict (store-wide), and an element naming no heat refuses the WHOLE call.
Stabled and retired heats are in the walk, their rows carrying the heat status.
Every result carries the STAMP — the studbook position the read rode — so a repeated call can tell a store that has not moved from a read that did not happen.
TWO FURTHER SECTIONS ride the poort roster's election-ignoring pattern, "(none)" when empty: DESIGNATIONS DUE — abeyant paces whose trigger resolved or answers no live pace, each row with the trigger's fate and the deferral's stamp, the banked brief indented whole beneath — and UNEXAMINED FRONTIER — rough, runnable on the graph, no deferral, hackamore and entrepas excluded, poort kept, and standing in a RACING heat — the racing subset of what jjx_apostille would admit, since the designation door gates nothing on heat status: a stabled heat's rough pace stands designable but owes no attention until its furlough.
A pending abeyant pace lands in NEITHER (parked, not forgotten); the two blocks form a net nothing designation-owed falls through

## `jjx_apostille`

THE DESIGNATION DOOR, three forms and exactly one per call.
Designate {coronet, tier, effort?}: only a rough pace, or an abeyant one whose trigger has RESOLVED — the conditional door admits and CONSUMES the deferral, announced; a pending trigger refuses, naming its standing and release as the supersede path; a lope is refused under its own sentence naming the kind and the jjx_whorl flip back.
Tiers haiku|sonnet|opus|fable, efforts low|medium|high|xhigh|max.
Defer {coronet, trigger}: parks a rough pace ABEYANT until the trigger pace (any coronet form, cross-heat licensed, not the subject, must resolve to a live pace) resolves — no tier or effort ride this form, a LEDE-LESS jjezs_abeyance brief is REQUIRED (a warrant staged here, or a brief staged against the tier form, refuses naming the form it belongs to), the recording commit carries the chalk trailer `Abeyance: <trigger body>`, and a re-deferral of an abeyant pace is a whole-replace, announced.
Release {coronet, release: true}: indult OR abeyant → rough, the standing form wiped (tier/effort/warrant, or brief/trigger) and named in the announcement; a lope is refused here too, naming the kind and the flip.
Next-actionable SKIPS an abeyant pace while its trigger pends and admits it once resolved; the fettle's DESIGNATIONS DUE block is where a due deferral surfaces.
A deferral voids exactly as a designation does — docket, sire, kind, transfer, relocate wipe it; silks and hopple never do.

## `jjx_rasp`

normalize-and-report — exit 0 clean / 2 normalized (rewrote+committed) / 1 broken (untouched); size_limit bounds the normalize commit, raised for the whole-store conversion a breaking schema change owes.
Read off the record and journaled by git, so nothing is lost — but a `2` on a store predating the live-graph doors is a real edit, not a re-indent.

## `jjx_refit`

merge a trunk that moved under your billet and a corpus trunk that moved under your vulgate billet — both axes, one enfold; see "Where a spec edit goes".
Open to every tier: it proves the merge first, so a clean one proceeds and announces.
A conflicted one refuses a sub-frontier caller with nothing merged and nothing moved, and lands git's markers for a frontier one.
MARKERS ARE NOT THE SETTLE FOR EVERY FILE: a GENERATED artifact colliding is re-rendered on the merged tree, never hand-merged — both sides are stale output of sources the merge already reconciled — and the refusal names those files apart with the render door each one's own header declares.
AHEAD OF BOTH MERGES IT REFUSES A SKUNKED KRAAL: where the pace's read list has moved since the dispatch that stood this kraal up, the peers standing no longer match what the pace declares plus what registration adds, and refit refuses hard naming both directions of the difference, having merged nothing and moved nothing.
It repairs nothing on that axis by ruling — standing a peer up and reaping one are the dispatch door's acts — so the remedy it names is the REMOUNT.

## `jjx_riem`

READ-ONLY sire report; no heat grain (a firemark refuses).
With coronet = report that pace's affiliation AND the read list it declares, the other half of its atajo, printed beneath — an UNDECLARED list is SAID rather than left silent, a pace declaring nothing standing no read-only peer at all; absent = the audit of every blank, grouped by heat.
Inline, no gazette, which is what makes it the cheap read before a designation.
The write retired into jjx_whorl's sire and readable_sires fields
