# JJK Portolan — What Each Door Does

Read this before you drive a `jjx_` verb you have not driven this session, and
whenever the question is what a door *returns* rather than what it *takes*. It
is an **excursus** (`jjsuc_excursus`): held out of the standing session load and
read on demand, reached from the trigger line beneath the MCP Command Reference
in `Tools/jjk/jjai0/jjais_jjk.md`.

A portolan is the written sailing directions that accompany a chart. The chart
marks each harbour; the portolan says what you find on entering one. The command
reference is the chart — every command's params stand there and are the
contract, an unknown field being named back to you with the fields the door
expected. What each door then *does* stands here: what prints inline and what
rides the gazette, what a param elects or filters, what the door announces, and
what it deliberately leaves alone.

## What stayed on the chart

Three classes of sentence kept their seat in the command reference rather than
moving here, because no refusal will tell you about them after the fact:

- a **destructive default** — an omitted param that widens the act, as
  `jjx_record` with no `files` does, and the whole-body replaces at `jjx_curry`,
  `jjx_rodear`, `jjx_enface` and `jjx_indite`;
- a **silent loss** — work that goes with every door reporting success: the
  cocket guard, a settled pace's demoted notch, a refit voiding criteria already
  driven, `jjx_rasp`'s normalize;
- a **fence** — an act that is the operator's to direct, which the equerry
  performs willingly and nothing refuses: `jjx_create`, `jjx_archive`,
  `jjx_rodear`.

Refusals themselves moved here, and that is the one place this file's shape
departs from a plain size cut. A refusal fires loudly, names what it expected
and names its own remedy, so the door corrects you at the moment the correction
is cheap. A silent loss has no such moment. When the always-loaded block could not
hold both classes under the composed-size ceiling, the loud half moved and the
quiet half stayed.

## How to read an entry

One heading per command, in the command reference's own order, carrying that
command's prose exactly as it stood in the fenced block — a passage moved whole,
nothing reworded in transit, one sentence per line.

Five commands have no entry: `jjx_muster`, `jjx_rail`, `jjx_relocate` and
`jjx_drop` never carried prose beyond their signatures, and `jjx_create` carries
only its fence, which stayed in the standing load.

## Every result stands whole in the gazette

This holds at every door, so no entry below repeats it. Every call the equerry
admits writes its result whole to `gazette_out.md` under a lede-less
`# jjezs_tenor` notice — success or refusal, at every size — standing last,
after whatever notices the door itself wrote; an entry below names only those.
Inline, the result arrives whole when it fits under the ceiling, and otherwise
as its leading whole lines and one closing line saying it was cut, its whole
size, and where it stands. Nothing is refused for its size. A setter drops that
notice from a staged gazette, so a reading's gazette renamed into `gazette_in.md`
carries straight into a write.

## `jjx_strigil`

THE CHAT CURATION DOOR, two forms in one command; its contract is `JJSCST-strigil.adoc`.
THE READ FORM (write absent or false) CHANGES NOTHING and is open to every session: it brings the station's chat line home, sweeps the harness folders whose sessions stood under the infield — or under `legacy_root`, an absolute directory of the era before the infield stood — weighs every chat against the line, reads every byte a write would land for the rostered secret shapes, and reports each hit (`hit <uuid> <file>:<offset> <shape> <digest>`), each divergence (`divergence <uuid> <offset> <file>`), each unread file, each uuid doubled across folders, the chats the retention floor sets aside, and the plan with its batch count. A staged notice or a `retention` value refuses here.
THE WRITE FORM ({write: true}) FUNCTIONS ONLY DURING A POORT: the seated pace's kind is the whole gate, and any other seat meets an INTERDICTUM naming the slate of a poort pace (JJr_vym).
Past the gate it applies `retention` — a YYYY-MM-DD floor, or empty to turn retention off; this door is the field's one setter — sweeps exactly as the read form with no deadline on the fetch, applies the rulings, and pushes the clean chats onto the line in batches, one fast-forward each, the ledger (`<tincture>/placita`) riding the first; it closes on one entry of the studbook's append-only branch under the lock, whose trailer names the line and the tip the write left on it.
RULINGS RIDE A LEDE-LESS jjezs_placitum NOTICE, one per line: `acquittal <shape> <digest>` once that secret is rotated, `acceptance <uuid> <offset>` ruling a rewritten source the new truth, `decline <uuid>` holding a chat back from this one write; the shape and digest are copied off the read form's hit line. Acquittals and acceptances enter the ledger; a decline enters nothing.
REFUSES, landing nothing, while retention stands off — save the call whose own value turned it off, which lands nothing and closes on its entry — while any chat it would land carries an unruled hit, an unaccepted divergence or an unread file and was not declined, and where a ruling names a hit, divergence or chat the sweep reports nowhere.
A killed run is resumed by running it again, the comparison being the state; a run that lands nothing and sets nothing writes no entry, save a station's first curation, which always writes one.
`archive` NAMES ONE HOST TREE of the founding chat archive by its name beneath `chat-archive/`, never a path, and turns either form to it: the read form classes each chat of that tree against the live harness store — `absent`, `identical`, `prefix` or `divergent <uuid> <folder>`, one line apiece — reads the tree's every byte for the shapes, its hits naming the archive path, and reports each entry that is no chat (`unsorted <path>`), each uuid doubled, and the absent chats the retention floor sets aside; the write form folds the absent chats alone onto the line by placing the archive's own copies rather than writing them again, leaves a chat whose path the line already holds otherwise where it stands (`standing <uuid> <path>`), takes acquittals and declines but no acceptance, the arm folding no divergence, and closes as any write does. The archive itself is never moved or removed

## `jjx_hansard`

THE CHAT TENANT READ BACK, two forms in one command, exactly one argument per call; its contract is `JJSCHN-hansard.adoc`.
Open to every session at every tier: it takes no lock, writes nothing to the store, and never writes the studbook clone or brings another station's line into it — every line is read through a scratch store of its own, fetched shallow and without blobs, and removed when the call ends.
`{coronet}` — THE ROSTER FORM: one row per tenure sighted on the pace, in sighting order — the laager, the sighting time, the model, the chat uuid, and the line holding that chat by its station's tincture, `on no line yet`, or every line where more than one holds it. Where the transport does not carry, every row still answers from the store and one line says why the lines went unread.

## `jjx_apostille`

A WARRANT IS REQUIRED ON EVERY DESIGNATION, staged per the gazette table below, never a param.

Designate {coronet, tier, effort?}: only a rough pace, or an abeyant one whose trigger has RESOLVED — the conditional door admits and CONSUMES the deferral, announced; a pending trigger refuses, naming its standing and release as the supersede path; a lope is refused under its own sentence naming the kind and the jjx_whorl flip back.

## `jjx_refit`

merge a trunk that moved under your billet and a vulgate that moved under your vulgate billet — both axes, one enfold; see "Where a spec edit goes".
Open to every tier: it proves the merge first, so a clean one proceeds and announces.
A conflicted one refuses a sub-frontier caller with nothing merged and nothing moved, and lands git's markers for a frontier one.
MARKERS ARE NOT THE SETTLE FOR EVERY FILE: a GENERATED artifact colliding is never hand-merged — both sides are stale output of sources the merge already reconciled — and the refusal names those files apart with the render door each one's own header declares.
Settle its markers by taking either side's bytes whole, complete the merge through `jjx_record` with the rest of the settled tree, then run its render door on the merged tree and notch what it changes.
AHEAD OF BOTH MERGES IT REFUSES A SKUNKED KRAAL: where the pace's read list has moved since the dispatch that stood this kraal up, the peers standing no longer match what the pace declares plus what registration adds, and refit refuses hard naming both directions of the difference, having merged nothing and moved nothing.
It repairs nothing on that axis by ruling — standing a peer up and reaping one are the dispatch door's acts — so the remedy it names is the saddle's resume.
THE THIRD AXIS IS THE READ-ONLY TREES, a fast-forward and never a merge: behind both merges it advances each sumpter beside the kraal to its sire trunk's remote counterpart as the elected clone knows it, so a sire's unpushed work stays invisible.
In a groom kraal — no billet, so neither merge — the lectern's refresh stands first and the sumpter arm runs behind it, only where that refresh landed; a lectern refusal leaves every sumpter untouched.
The sumpter arm is soft: a sumpter it cannot advance — an unreachable sire, a failed fetch, a position not descending from the standing one, a tree holding changes — is named untouched at its position and the refit keeps its report, and every sumpter is named by handle and position whether it moved or stood.
A sumpter's pin is set by its dispatch at birth and moves afterwards only by this verb.
